# Original discovery apps

Mode: Operate. Extend prettTV's established native Android TV world. The owner requested Blind Pick for movies and TV, plus Rabbit Hole, launched only from the bottom Library. Keep Home and the top app row unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Turn available catalog information into a deliberate choice or an explainable connection.

OWN-WORLD: Existing sage-black surfaces, ivory focus, system sans-serif, subtle card edges and colorful original app marks. Library uses one #1F2A22 surface, #647368 edges, an ivory 24-pixel label and a contained 64-pixel-high logo area. Blind Pick uses three cards and a reveal spark; Rabbit Hole uses a rabbit emerging from a portal.

STORY: Blind Pick filters three anonymous choices and reveals one on demand. Rabbit Hole starts with a real title and labels connections from actual genres or verified credits.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title and Back above native filters; three wide mystery cards for Blind Pick. Rabbit Hole places its starting title above relationship filters and related cards.

FORM: Local extension of the pinned TV card and row system; fixed composition follows the approved feature concepts, so no new visual-world roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

No network work on the UI thread, background services, new accounts, invented availability or fabricated creative relationships. Mystery cards must hide titles and artwork from accessibility as well as sight. Respect hidden titles, recommendation feedback, service priorities and Stremio-last ordering. Missing metadata produces an honest empty state.

Deployment is deferred to the user's October 20, 2026, 4–5 AM Eastern window. Native TV validation is pending until that window. Mockups contain clearly labeled sample content.
