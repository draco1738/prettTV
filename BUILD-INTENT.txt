prettTV implementation brief, 2026-10-17

Native Android TV launcher derived from Rivi with attribution. User direction: PLUI's panels, Rivi's lean rendering, useful stock-TV content rows. Mode: Operate using a TV remote.

A Cinema, B Quiet and C Balanced share a hidden left sidebar. C is the owner's selected Home. The hero uses the latest locally confirmed viewing record, with shared provider timestamps as the initial baseline. Below it: finite Your apps row, Continue watching Shows and Movies, Live sports, Suggestions, Trending, New releases and the full Library.

App hiding affects Your apps only; Library, search and viewing history retain the app. Title hiding remains independent for hero and Continue watching. Empty or unsupported sources must be described honestly.

Near-black olive-charcoal surfaces, warm ivory focus, restrained color accents and subtle card borders. Branding and credits are at the bottom. Bundled attributed app marks render offline on dark tiles; other apps supply their artwork. No live background blur or video wallpaper.

RBTV is included in the default companion installation bundle. Optional playback and bounded RBTV matching helpers require Android special access and retain explicit coverage limits. Installers do not grant special access or disable system Home apps.

Performance target is responsive 1080p navigation with bounded artwork work and lifecycle cleanup; no sustained-frame-rate promise. Validation and actual TV captures are documented in docs/validation.md.
