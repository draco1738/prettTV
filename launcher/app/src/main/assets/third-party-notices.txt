Optional Trending and New releases use Stremio's public [Cinemeta catalog](https://v3-cinemeta.strem.io/manifest.json), with artwork URLs supplied by the catalog (including Metahub). prettTV reformats those records and links titles to the installed Stremio app. Cinemeta's Popular order supplies Trending; it is not an independently measured trend score. New releases uses supplied release dates within the past 120 days. These requests disclose the device's IP and requested catalog/artwork identifiers to those services. This project does not use WatchHub's restricted availability API. The purple S badge identifies Stremio destinations.

## ESPN sports schedules

Live and upcoming fixtures use ESPN's public scoreboard endpoints, including https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/scoreboard . Network logos come from URLs supplied by ESPN. These are runtime data/artwork requests; no ESPN implementation is bundled. The endpoint is not a contracted API and may change. Schedules, scores, team/network names and logos remain the property of their respective owners. There is no endorsement. Responses are cached briefly; stale entries are labeled and expire after 15 minutes. No stream URLs, account data, or playback entitlement is supplied by this integration.

## Weather — NOAA / National Weather Service

Optional header weather uses the [National Weather Service API](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api) for nearby station observations. prettTV selects an available observation, converts and rounds its temperature for display, and retains the observation time. These are runtime data requests; no NWS software or logos are bundled. NWS information is generally public domain unless otherwise identified; its [data-use statement](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer) describes reuse and third-party exceptions. prettTV is independent of NOAA/NWS and does not present its display as an official government product.
